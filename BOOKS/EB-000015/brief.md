# Brief — EB-000015

## Working title
**Large Print Sudoku for Seniors** — 9 Easy to Hard Puzzles

## Audience
Readers aged roughly 60+ who want comfortably large, easy-to-read sudoku grids, plus beginners
being introduced to the puzzle by a family member. They are frustrated by puzzle books with small
print and by puzzles that turn out to have more than one valid solution.

## Promise
Every puzzle in this book has exactly one solution, independently verified before printing, in
large, easy-to-read print, sorted from easy to hard with a full solution key in the back.

## Differentiation
From research: most large-print puzzle books only claim "large print" without any checkable
quality guarantee. This book's puzzles are generated and re-verified by a solver (see
`manuscript/puzzles.json`), a concrete, checkable claim rather than marketing language.

## Outline
1. **Introduction** — objective: set expectations (count, verified uniqueness, difficulty order,
   solution key location); no exercise.
2. **How to Solve** — objective: teach the one sudoku rule and basic scanning technique for
   readers who are new to it; no exercise (reference chapter).
3. **Easy Puzzles** — the easy-tier puzzle grids, one per page.
4. **Medium Puzzles** — the medium-tier puzzle grids, one per page.
5. **Hard Puzzles** — the hard-tier puzzle grids, one per page.
6. **Solutions** — full solution key, same order as the puzzles.

## Tone
Plain, warm, reassuring. No jargon. Short sentences in the instructional chapters.

## Length
Intro + How to Solve only: approximately 600 words of prose (this is a short reference section,
not a full chapter — most of the book's value is the verified puzzles, not the instructions). The
rest of the book is puzzle grids and a solution key, not prose — word count does not apply there.

## Sources to use
None restated (see research.md: this run deliberately avoids citing third-party statistics).

## Risk
LOW. No health, legal, or financial claims; only the public-domain sudoku rule set is taught.

## Out of scope
Puzzle types other than sudoku; any claim about cognitive or health benefits of puzzle-solving.
